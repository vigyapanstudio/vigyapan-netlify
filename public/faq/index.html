Frequently asked questions

Short answers about ordering, your dashboard and how everything works. Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

Ordering and payment
What exactly do I get?

A PDF report on your Instagram page, a look at competitors, a 30-day calendar with a post, reel or story idea for every day (with captions and hashtags), and a short video walkthrough. Everything also stays saved in your dashboard.

How long does it take?

Within 48 hours of your payment being confirmed. We message you on WhatsApp if we need anything, for example if your account is private.

How do I pay?

Fill in the form on the home page and pay on the next step with UPI, card, netbanking or wallet. Payments are handled by Razorpay, so we never see or store your card or UPI details.

What is the 3-month plan?

You pay once for three months and get a fresh report and 30-day calendar at the start of each month. It costs ₹3,999, which saves ₹498 compared with paying ₹1,499 three times. Prices can change without prior notice.

What are the 10 reel scripts?

An optional add-on for ₹499. You get ten word-for-word reel scripts in Hinglish or English, written for your business, so you know exactly what to say on camera.

Will I get an invoice?

Yes. A receipt and invoice are emailed to you right after payment, and you can open any invoice again from your dashboard.

Your plan and dashboard
How do I log in to my dashboard?

Go to the Log in page and enter the email you used to order. We send a 6-digit code to that email. There is no password to remember. Check your spam folder if the code does not arrive within a minute.

How do I renew?

Open your dashboard and tap Renew. You can choose one month or three months. There is no automatic charge, so you only pay when you choose to. If you renew early, your new days start when your current plan ends, so no days are wasted.

What is the posting tracker?

In your dashboard you will see your 30 days laid out with dates. Tick each day when you have posted and watch your progress bar fill up. If we have added day-by-day ideas for your page, they appear next to each day.

Will you remind me before my plan ends?

Yes. We email you five days before, one day before, and after your plan ends, so you can renew without a gap.

Do you need my Instagram password?

Never. We only look at your public page, so we only need your handle. Please do not share your password with anyone.

Will this get me followers or sales?

The plan shows you what to post and when, based on what works in your market. Results depend on how consistently you follow it, so we do not promise any number of followers or sales.

Referrals and free tips
How does the referral code work?

Every customer gets a personal code in their dashboard. A friend who types it at checkout gets 10% off their first order. When their payment goes through, you get a 10% credit that is applied automatically on your next renewal.

Can I use a referral code on a renewal?

Referral codes are for first orders only. Your own 10% credit is what applies on a renewal.

What are the free tips?

If you are not ready to buy, send us your Instagram handle on the free audit page. We look at your page and message you 3 quick, specific things to improve on WhatsApp, usually within 24 hours. It is free and there is no obligation.
