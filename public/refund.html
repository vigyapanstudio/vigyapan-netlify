Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We want you to be happy with your report. This policy explains when you can cancel or get your money back.

Monthly renewals

The same rules apply to every monthly renewal. Each renewal is a separate order with its own 48-hour delivery and 7-day refund window.

Cancel before we start

If you cancel before we begin work on your report, you get a full refund. Message us on WhatsApp or email as soon as you change your mind. We will tell you on WhatsApp when we start your report.

7-day refund after delivery

If you do not find your report useful, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will refund the amount you paid for that order. Please message us on WhatsApp (+91 70098 84880) or email vigyapanstudio@gmail.com and tell us what was missing, so we can improve.

If we cannot deliver

If we cannot complete your report (for example, your Instagram account stays private or the handle is wrong and cannot be fixed), you get a full refund.

What is not covered

  • Requests made more than 7 days after delivery.
  • The results you get on Instagram. The report is a plan, and we do not guarantee followers, views, enquiries or sales.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the same payment method you used (UPI, card or bank). Once we approve a refund it usually reaches you within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

Delivery

This is a digital service, so nothing is shipped. Your report is delivered by WhatsApp and/or email within 48 hours of payment confirmation.
