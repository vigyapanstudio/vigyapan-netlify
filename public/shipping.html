Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Vigyapan Studio sells a digital service: an Instagram Growth Report and 30-Day Content Calendar (and, if you choose it, 10 reel scripts). Nothing physical is shipped, so there are no shipping charges and no courier.

How you receive your report

We send your report (a PDF, plus the calendar and any add-on scripts) on the WhatsApp number and/or the email address you gave us when you placed your order. Please check that both are correct before you pay.

Delivery time

We deliver within 48 hours of your payment being confirmed (the Quick Page Check is delivered within 24 hours). If we need more information from you (for example, your Instagram account is private or the handle is wrong), the 48 hours start once we have it. We will message you on WhatsApp if we need anything.

Delivery area

Because the service is digital, we can deliver anywhere you can receive WhatsApp or email. Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).

If you have not received your report

If more than 48 hours have passed (and we have not asked you for anything), message us on WhatsApp (+91 70098 84880) or email vigyapanstudio@gmail.com with your name and the order details on your receipt. We will look into it and fix it.
