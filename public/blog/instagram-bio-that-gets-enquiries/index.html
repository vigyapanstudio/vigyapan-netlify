How to write an Instagram bio that gets enquiries

By Nipun Aggarwal, Vigyapan Studio · October 2026 · 4 min read

When someone finds your reel and taps your name, your bio is what decides if they follow, message you or leave. You only have a few seconds and 150 characters, so every word has to work.

The four parts of a good bio

  1. Who you help. Say it plainly, for example "Bridal makeup for brides in Ludhiana".
  2. What you do or what makes you different. One short line: "Natural looks that last all day".
  3. Proof or location. Years of experience, number of customers served, or your area.
  4. One clear action. "Book on WhatsApp" or "Order from the link below".

Examples you can adapt

  • "Home bakery in Zirakpur. Custom cakes for birthdays and weddings. Orders open daily. Message us to order."
  • "Personal training for busy professionals in Mohali. Strength and fat loss. 8 years of coaching. Book a free trial."
  • "Boutique for festive and bridal wear. New designs every Friday. Visit us in Chandigarh or DM to shop."

Fix the name field too

The "Name" line at the top of your profile (not your username) is searchable. Instead of just your brand name, add what you do, for example "Glow Salon | Bridal Makeup Ludhiana". That helps people find you when they search for the service.

Use the link and the buttons

  • Put one useful link in your bio. A WhatsApp chat link or a page with your menu, prices or booking works well.
  • Turn on the contact buttons (call, email, directions) on a professional account.
  • Pin your best three posts so a new visitor sees your strongest work first.
  • Use Highlights for the questions you get most: prices, location, how to order, reviews.

Check it with fresh eyes

Show your profile to someone who does not know your business and ask: what do we sell, who is it for, and what should I do next? If they cannot answer in five seconds, rewrite the bio until they can.
