Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Vigyapan Studio ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect when you visit this website or order from us, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Order details you type in: your name, WhatsApp number, email, Instagram handle, business name and type, city, follower range, goal, competitor handles, and any notes you add.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive confirmation of the payment (such as a payment ID and amount), but we never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • Your account: when you buy a plan we create a dashboard for you using your email. We store your details, your payment history (payment IDs and amounts), the notes you write for next month, and the reports and calendars we prepare for you. When you log in we email you a one-time code, and we keep a single cookie in your browser to remember that you are logged in. We do not use that cookie for advertising.
  • Free audit requests: if you ask for free tips, we store your name, WhatsApp number, Instagram handle, business type and email (if you add one) so we can message you the tips.
  • Feedback and referrals: if you leave a review we store your rating, text and the name you choose to show; we show it on our website only if you tick the box and we approve it. We also keep a personal referral code for each customer and a record of discounts and credits used.
  • Your posting progress: the days you tick off in your dashboard calendar.
  • Website usage: basic information such as pages visited and device type, collected through the Meta (Facebook) Pixel if it is switched on, so we can measure how our ads perform.

Why we use it

  • To prepare and deliver your report and calendar, and to contact you about your order on WhatsApp or email.
  • To run your dashboard: log you in, show your reports and invoices, and email you receipts, invoices, report-ready notices and reminders before your month ends.
  • To process payments, apply discounts and keep your posting tracker.
  • To send you the free tips you asked for, and to ask for your feedback about a week after your first report is delivered.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and website.
  • To keep records we need for accounts and tax.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with services we use to run the business: Razorpay (payments), our website host and file storage (Netlify), our email sender (Resend), Meta (advertising measurement) and WhatsApp (messaging with you). They process it only to provide their service.

How long we keep it

We keep your order details for as long as needed to deliver and support your order, and for the period required for accounting and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing vigyapanstudio@gmail.com. Records we must keep by law may be kept for the required period. You can also control ad tracking in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings.

Children

Our service is meant for business owners aged 18 and over.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
